About the game

How it works

A shift on the crane, start to finish.

Clock in

Punch your card at the site's time clock. Your foreman takes the cab and the hook starts to swing.

Build

Drop modules, keep them centred and ride the sway. Overhangs get cut off, and Perfect drops widen the module again.

Get paid

Lose all three hard hats and the shift ends. Your points arrive in a pay envelope as bolts to spend on upgrades.

Signature mechanics

Six systems that work together, so no two towers turn out the same.

Sway rhythm

The tower sways more the higher it gets. Land a module while the top passes the centre to be In Tune and calm it. Off-beat landings rock it until it collapses.

Rivet strike

After each landing, a ring closes in on the joint. Tap as it hits the mark to rivet the floor for double points. When the tower collapses, only floors above the last rivet fall.

Tenant districts

Stack three Residential, Office or Sky Garden floors in a row to form a district. Districts pay points or bolts, or halve the sway. A Penthouse rivets every floor below it.

Wind & Rush

The weathervane warns you before a gust pushes the hook. Quick drops build Rush, a multiplier of up to x2, but the hook swings faster too.

Cable snap & site shifts

A creaking cable will drop the module by itself. Land it perfectly anyway for a Cable Catch. Every ten floors a new shift starts: fog, night, a jumpy crane, steel girders or dead calm.

Trim & regrow

Any overhang is cut off and tumbles away. Land four Perfect drops in a row and the module widens again.

The crew & the floors

Six foremen, each with a perk that changes how you play.

Weekly building league

Your tallest tower each week decides your league. The Top 5 crews compete for exclusive crane and roof details: a Gold Leaf Jib, a Neon Crown and a Chrome Hook.

How do I rivet a floor?

Right after a module lands, a ring closes in on the joint. Tap again the moment it meets the yellow mark. A riveted floor scores double and survives collapses.

Why did my tower collapse?

Too much sway. Watch the plumb bob in the bottom-left corner: land while it passes through the green band to calm the tower, and form Sky Garden districts to halve the sway.

Do I need an internet connection?

No. The whole game plays offline. The weekly league board only refreshes when you are online.

Are there ads or purchases?

No. Bolts are earned only by playing, and there is nothing to buy with real money.

How do I start over?

Open the Site Office from the hoist pendant and use Reset progress. This permanently clears bolts, upgrades, foremen and the shift log.

When do weekly orders and the league reset?

Every Monday at 00:00 UTC. Each site order pays once per week.

Privacy Policy

Effective 17 November 2026

Rivet Rise is designed to collect as little as possible.

Data we collect

The app does not require a personal account and contains no ads. Adjust may process install attribution to measure campaign performance. The app stores your local progress (bolts, upgrades, foremen, shift history and settings) on your device.

Weekly league

When you open the weekly league, Rivet Rise sends an anonymous app identity to Supabase so it can keep your crew profile and weekly results. Completed shifts send your floors, score, rivets, perfect drops, districts, duration and chosen foreman so the board can rank your crew. Your crew name is generated by the app. Rivet Rise does not upload your local save file.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.
